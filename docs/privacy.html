Privacy Policy — Session Commander

Effective date: September 29, 2026.

1. The short version

Session Commander collects nothing. The tool runs entirely on your machine, reads your local coding-agent session files, and sends no data anywhere — no telemetry, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts. The only network connection the tool ever makes is to Lemon Squeezy's license API when you activate a Pro license key (and periodic re-validation afterwards).

2. What the software itself collects

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No usage telemetry or analytics of any kind.
  • No crash reports, no device fingerprints, no advertising identifiers.
  • Your session transcripts, costs, and rules files never leave your machine.
  • License activation sends only: the license key you entered, a machine instance name (hostname + username, used to count your 5 activations), and the activation request itself — to api.lemonsqueezy.com. This is required for the license to work and happens only in connection with activation/validation.

You can verify this: the entire source is public at github.com/baby-zack-agent/session-commander, has zero dependencies, and the only URL it contacts is Lemon Squeezy's license API.

3. What Lemon Squeezy collects (purchases)

When you buy Session Commander Pro, Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and processes your payment. It collects the information needed to complete the transaction (name, email, payment details, billing country for tax). That data is governed by Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy, not this one. We receive only what Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers: typically your email address and order/license details, which we use solely to fulfill and support your license.

4. What this website collects

These legal pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. We run no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking on them. GitHub (the host) may log standard server data per its own privacy statement.

5. Email

If you email us for support, we keep your message only as long as needed to resolve your issue. We do not operate a marketing mailing list; we will never add you to one.

6. Your rights (EU/UK and others)

Because we collect essentially nothing, there is little to access or delete — but you still have the right to ask. If you believe we hold personal data about you (e.g., a support email), contact us and we will provide it, correct it, or delete it. EU/UK residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their data protection authority.

7. Children

Session Commander is a developer tool not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
